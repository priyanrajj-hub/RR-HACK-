## 🔬 Scientific Methodology

1. **Pressure Index ($PI \in [0, 1]$):**
   $$PI = w_{chase} \cdot P_{chase} + w_{coll} \cdot P_{coll} + w_{phase} \cdot L_{phase} + w_{stakes} \cdot S_{match}$$
   - **Innings 2 Chase Pressure:** Required Run Rate ($RRR$) vs Current Run Rate ($CRR$), amplified as remaining wickets decrease and overs run down.
   - **Innings 1 Collapse Pressure:** Excess wickets lost relative to par ($\mu_W = 0.28$ wkts/over) plus rolling collapse indicator ($\ge 2$ wickets in preceding 18 balls).
   - **Phase Leverage:** Death overs 16–20 weighted at 0.90; Middle overs 7–15 at 0.45; Powerplay at 0.35.
   - **Match Stakes:** Knockout and playoff fixtures receive a +0.20 stakes multiplier.

2. **Empirical Distribution Proof:**
   At the 72nd percentile cutoff ($PI \ge 0.4442$):
   - **Wicket Hazard:** Jumps by **+68.1%** (from 4.54% to 7.63% per ball, $p < 0.0001$).
   - **Scoring Profile:** Dot ball pressure increases, forcing higher boundary dependence (18.6% vs 17.7%).

3. **Context-Expected Performance Baselines:**
   - **Batters:** Evaluated on Runs Scored Above Expected ($RSAE$) and Survival Delta ($SRD$) against phase- and bowler-type conditioned baselines.
   - **Bowlers:** Evaluated on Runs Saved Above Expected ($RSAE$) and Bowling Strike Rate Delta, isolating bowler credit by excluding fielding extras (byes/leg-byes) and non-bowler dismissals (run-outs).

4. **Competition Tier Multipliers:**
   - **IPL (Tier 1):** 1.30x weight (pinnacle global bowling quality).
   - **Tier-1 T20I (Top 8):** 1.10x weight.
   - **Associate / Emerging Nations:** 0.65x weight.

5. **Empirical Bayes Shrinkage:**
   Regresses observed ratings toward replacement population mean ($\mu = 50.0$) using prior weight $K = 50$ deliveries to eliminate small-sample survivor bias:
   $$S_{Bayes} = \frac{n}{n + K} \cdot S_{obs} + \frac{K}{n + K} \cdot \mu$$
   Minimum eligibility: $\ge 120$ pressure balls and $\ge 15$ matches.
